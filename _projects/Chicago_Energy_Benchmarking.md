 - Designed and implemented a Big Data analytics pipeline to process and analyze large-scale energy consumption data from Chicago buildings using Hadoop and Spark.
 - Leveraged Apache Spark DataFrame APIs for distributed data processing, aggregation, and advanced analytical queries on energy benchmarking data.
 - Deployed and managed a containerized Hadoop ecosystem using Docker, including HDFS, YARN, Spark, and HBase services.
 - Performed exploratory data analysis and generated visualizations to identify trends in energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, and building performance.
 - Optimized distributed data workflows and validated processing results across Hadoop and Spark environments.
 - Delivered a complete Big Data analytics platform integrating Hadoop, Spark, HBase, and Docker to process, store, analyze, and visualize large-scale energy benchmarking data from Chicago buildings.
